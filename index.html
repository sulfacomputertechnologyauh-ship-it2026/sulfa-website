Saleculator POS Features

Retail Features

See the Saleculator capabilities that you’ll need at a retail counter.

Fast Product Lookups

Associating quick buttons to commonly sold items or using embedded barcodes, you handle any rush hour.

  • Quick Keys: Shortcuts that add commonly sold items with one tap. They can also be used for frequent tasks.
  • Embedded Barcodes: Barcodes that contain price, quantity, or weight—commonly used for weighing items, removing manual entry.
  • Quick Search: Instantly search or lookup products directly from the sell screen without changing views.

Multiple Payment Methods

Go beyond traditional payments, split bills, and accept partial payments seamlessly.

  • Variety of Payments: Accept cash, card, debit, advance, foreign currency, gift vouchers, cheques, etc.
  • Partial Payments: Accept more than one payment method per sale (e.g., part cash, part card).
  • Bill Splitting: Split a sale by items across any number of customers.

Tariff Sales

Tariffs or price books let you create special pricing for different customer groups.

  • Tariffs: Create multiple prices for an item, e.g., wholesale or discounted pricing for loyal customers.

Returns & Refunds

Process refunds or exchanges easily from the Edit Sales screen.

  • Refund a Sale: Refund full or partial sales; refunded items return to inventory.
  • Returns: Exchange items by editing a bill.

Cash Closing

Perform day-end or shift closing to balance your cash drawer and maintain accountability.

  • Cash Closing: Record cash and coins in the drawer, generate reports, and email summaries. Supports counter-wise and central closing.
  • Blind Closing: Cashiers enter actual cash without seeing expected balances, preventing manipulation.
  • Partial Closing: Generate sequence-numbered reports for shift handovers.

Inventory

Track stock, purchases, and product performance across locations.

  • Purchase: Save purchase invoices from POS and manage supplier payments.
  • Reorder Levels: Get alerts when stock runs low.
  • Stock Transfer: Transfer items easily between stores or warehouses.

Customers

Maintain customer records, allow credit sales, and build loyalty.

  • Quick Creation & Lookup: Add customers from the sales screen or scan loyalty cards.
  • Customer Credits: Enable debt sales and track pending balances with detailed customer diaries.
  • Customer Loyalty: Award loyalty points for purchases and allow redemption later.

Restaurant Features

Restaurants have specific needs that retail shops don’t have. Along with the Point of Sale features, here are the features your restaurant must have.

Floors & Tables

Associating quick buttons to commonly sold items or using embedded barcodes helps you handle any rush hour.

  • Quick Keys: Shortcuts that add a commonly sold item to the sales ticket; can also execute frequent tasks.
  • Embedded Barcodes: Carry item price, quantity, or weight, reducing manual entry for weighing items.
  • Quick Search: Lookup products directly from the sales screen. Floor and table management ensures best service and efficient workflow.
  • Floor & Table Management: Track customers from order to bill across multiple floors and tables for optimal dining experience.
  • Table Alerts: Real-time notifications for table status.
  • Table Shifting & Merging: Move or combine tables easily during service.

Kitchen Order Printing

Streamline orders from POS to kitchen efficiently.

  • Multiple Payment Methods: Accept cash, card, debt, advance, foreign currency, gift vouchers, cheques, etc.
  • Partial Payments: Accept multiple payment methods per sale; collect remaining balance later.
  • Bill Splitting: Quickly split sales by item across customers.

Bill Settlement & Tariffs

  • Bill Settlement: Apply special pricing for customers using tariffs or price books.
  • Tariffs: Create multiple prices for items based on customer category, wholesale, or loyalty discounts.

Costing

  • Refund a Sale: Refund full or partial sales; refunded items return to inventory.
  • Returns: Exchange items by editing bills instead of refunds.

Online & PDA Orders

Cash Closing

  • Blind Closing: Cashier enters actual counts without seeing expected balances, preventing manipulation.
  • Partial Closing: Generate reports without removing cash, ideal for shift handovers.
